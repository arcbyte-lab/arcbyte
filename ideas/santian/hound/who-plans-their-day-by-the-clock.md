## The question
Who already thinks about their day as blocks of time on a clock face, rather
than as a flat list of tasks?

## Why it matters
Every design note in this idea assumes the clockface is the point. There is no
input in this vault from a single real person. Until there is, the whole idea
rests on one developer's own habit.

## How I could answer it
- Cheapest way: ask five people how they planned yesterday. Do not mention the
  app. Just ask what they used and what went wrong.
- Best way: watch three people plan a day, and see whether they reach for time
  or for a list.
