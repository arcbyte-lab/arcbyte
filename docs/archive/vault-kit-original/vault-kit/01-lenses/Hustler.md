# Hustler — the one who gets paid

**Owns:** money, users, and survival.

This is the lens developers skip. It is also the one that decides whether a
project stays a nights-and-weekends toy or becomes something real. If an idea in
this vault has no hustler notes at all, that is a signal, not an oversight.

## What to ask it

- Who pays, and instead of what? What budget does this money come out of?
- How does the first stranger find out this exists?
- What is the one-line pitch? If I cannot say it in one line, the product is
  still blurry.
- Who else already solves this, and what would make someone switch?
- What is the smallest thing I could charge for today?

## What you can expect back

Uncomfortable arithmetic. A positioning line you will hate at first and use
later. A list of channels, most of which will not work.

## What it must not do

Promise features to win an argument. Set the design direction.

## Artifact kinds here

`positioning`, `pricing`, `channel`, `teardown`, `risk`, `analysis`

## Working with AI here

Good for competitor teardowns and for generating twenty positioning lines so you
can pick one. Bad at anything requiring a real number — market sizes it produces
are decoration. Mark those `evidence: none` and go find the real figure yourself
if the decision depends on it.
